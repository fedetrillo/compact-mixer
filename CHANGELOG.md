# Changelog

## 1.2.2 — 2026-11-25

- Screenshots of the dark and grey themes in the package and the README, and a shorter
  description that starts with what it does for you.

## 1.2.1 — 2026-11-25

### Added
- **Quick guide:** the first time it opens, a short guide walks you through the window in the
  language you choose first (seven languages). The **Guide** button in the transport bar brings
  it back.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-24

### Changed
- **New look**, in line with the newer add-ons: rounded strips, lanes and buttons, grouped
  transport, gradient highlights, a clock in the add-on's colour and softer pop-ups. Same layout
  and density: as many channels fit on screen as before.

### Added
- **Colour:** pick the add-on's colour under the Look button (◐): yellow, orange, red, green,
  blue, pink or purple. It tints the selected strip, the fader caps, the EQ curve, pan and send
  sliders and the active buttons, and is remembered with the theme.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-21

### Added
- **Plugins from the wide strip:** "+ Plugin" opens the plugins LUNA offers for that insert, by
  category, with search. A new plugin goes at the end of the chain.
- **Arrange inserts:** drag an insert onto another, or right-click it to move it up or down,
  replace, bypass or remove it.
- **Sends from the wide strip:** "+ Send" lists the session's buses or makes a new one; the send
  starts at -inf. Right-click a send to move it to another bus or remove it.
- **Fader groups 1-8:** faders in a group move together by the same dB. Mute, solo and reset to
  0 dB (Option/Alt-click or double-click) act on the whole group. Option/Alt-drag moves one fader
  alone; the Groups button suspends every group.
- **Mix snapshots:** save the mix (faders, mutes, solos, pans, send levels) under a name and
  recall it with one click, as one undo step.
- **Folders:** channels inside a folder carry a band in its colour; a folder folds and unfolds
  on narrow and wide strips.
- **Channel colours:** click the colour bar or right-click a name to pick one of 24 colours,
  for every selected channel at once.
- **Look:** a grey theme beside the dark one, and strips tinted with their channel's colour
  (0-60%).
- **A/B** speaker switch in the transport bar, working together with the AB Monitor Add On.
- **Multi-select:** Cmd/Ctrl-click and Shift-click channel names.
- Name and version in the top bar.
- User manual in English and Spanish (docs/Compact-Mixer-Manual.pdf).

### Changed
- The fader line is a little thicker.
- Removing a plugin closes its window first, so LUNA does not stall.

## 1.0.0

- First release: narrow strips whose lane is fader and meter, wide strips with inserts, EQ
  view, sends and pan, pinning, transport bar, LUNA Mixer button, audio-scale dB ruler.
